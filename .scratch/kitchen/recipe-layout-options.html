Espresso Brownies

Butter and flour the panPreheat oven to 350°F / 170°C
Ingredients enteringAction
  • 4 oz · 115 g unsalted butter
1
Melt the butter

Melt gently, then take it off the heat.

  • 1 cup · 200 g sugar
  • ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 shot · 60 ml strong coffee
2
Build the wet mixture

Stir sugar, vanilla, and espresso into the melted butter.

  • 2 large · 100 g eggs
3
Mix in the eggs

Add the eggs and mix until the batter looks smooth and glossy.

  • ½ cup · 80 g all-purpose flour
  • ⅓ cup · 80 g cocoa powder

  • ¼ tsp · 1.5 g table salt
4
Fold in the dry ingredients

Fold just until no dry streaks remain; avoid overmixing.

5
Bake until just set

Spread into the pan and bake until the centre is set with moist crumbs.
